## Reproduce

```
python3 -I scripts/verify_hashes.py
python3 -I scripts/measure.py key/table_T2.json transcriptions/H2.txt      # 0.8848, best control 0.2946 (about 2 min)
python3 -I scripts/controls.py key/table_T2.json transcriptions/H2.txt     # per-control scores
python3 -I scripts/measure.py key/table_T.json transcriptions/H_561.txt    # 0.7976, best control 0.3066
python3 -I scripts/units_573.py                                           # image 573 counts
python3 -I scripts/check_training.py
```

Measure: P = concatenated T2 values of the groups of each glossed passage (a group not in the key gives nothing), R = concatenated normalised glosses (lower case, accents removed, a-z only, abbreviations expanded as in addendum 1); score = 1 − Levenshtein(P, R) / max(|P|, |R|). Control k = the values of the key permuted among its keys with `random.Random(k)`, k = 1..200. One group at a time (the script does not apply the "longest sequence first" rule of section 2 of the protocol; no multi-group entry exists in T or T2, so it changes nothing).

## Image 573 (30 Nov 1665, pp. 2-3), counts

238 units. By sense: 220 sure, 13 probable, 5 unresolved, **233/238 = 97.90%**. Strict, this folder's rule: **S 173/238 = 72.69%**, E 43, N 22 (S + E = 216). Not a held-out test: T2 was validated on H2 before 573 was read, but the reading also uses the additions file. Four corrections to our 11 Nov sheet (247 words, 98.0%) are listed in `reading/573_reading.md` (61 is the Roy Très Chrestien, not France; 334, "tous" and "persuadés" now probable).

## Known limits

- The training transcription is post hoc (see above). The held-out transcriptions are frozen first passes by one reader; some glosses were read with doubt and kept as seen.
- The sign 9 stands for t, p or b; the film does not separate its forms, T2 has 9 = t. Strict counts take that value.
- 61 and 92 are seen in training; T2 restates them with the article from the 561 glosses (le roy …); the measure compares letters, so the article counts.
- 88: our three explicit glosses against the short gloss of 574 right, see `notes/value_88.md`.
