## 14 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1048–1050

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts, headed 186 and 171, one part per frame. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1048 (SRO 0132, accepted 15.35, passed on 16.55 and 17.00; Briefbuch-Nr. F 141/40 g.Kdos). It is the only message of the day on the reel.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (22)  13 18 2 8 20 12 3 16 19 5 22 10 17 6 1 14 7 11 4 21 9 15
B (27)  9 23 20 14 27 10 16 1 24 12 6 17 3 26 7 19 13 2 21 8 15 4 18 11 5 22 25
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 347 letters. One letter on the sheet does not make its word: FENERBEREIT, an n written without the breve this writer puts on u.

**What it says.** II. Zug 4./491 moved on 13 May from Titisee to Hausach station and has been ready to fire since 05.00 on 14 May, protecting army unit 701. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day.

**Method (design S2).** There is no second message to hold out, so both parts were searched together and the commander's name in the signature (FLUGABWEHRKOMMANDOSCHWARZWALD, 29 letters) was held back from the crib as the hold-out. To fit a short message, the search settings were changed beforehand: key lengths 15–30 and a stage-1 margin of 1.10. Both changes were chosen in twelve rehearsal runs on days already solved, all filed with the result. The crib was cut at the hand-drawn part break and fitted to the letter counts before the seal (L1 1 and 5).

**Prereg.** Hash `ba641b1ca94a4399bcd7a1c9d6603c0685d54f5aece8251ffe410a46f9f7d8f6` (12 Nov 2026 23:52:29 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 602 against a decoy ceiling of 384 (1.57 times, above the 1.25 bar of the design for longer messages too).
- The search text agreed at 0.896, with 11 of 12 words.
- The held-back name decrypts at 0.966, against 0.345 for the best of 200 random key pairs; FLUGABW and WARZWALD both read letter for letter.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 285 letters, against 24.

**Reading.** None of the 347 letters first read was a misreading.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 1048, SRO 0132, accepted 14 May 1940 15.35, passed on 16.55 and 17.00)

*Radio text, with word division*
GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE X ROEM X ZWEI X ZUG X VIER X STRIQ X VIERNEUNEINS X HAT AM X EINSDREI X FUENF X VIERNULL X STELLUNGSWEQSEL VON X TITISEE X NAQ BAHNHOF X HAUSACH X DURQGEFUEHRT UND IST SEIT X EINSVIER X FUENF X VIERNULL X ‖ X NULLFUENFNULLNULL X UHR ZUM SQUTZE DER HEERESEINHEIT X SIEBENNULLEINS X FE[U/N]ERBEREIT X FLUGABWEHRKOMMANDO X SCHWARZWALD X ROEM X EINS X CAESAR X NR X FRITZ X EINSVIEREINS X STRIQ X VIERNULL X GKDOS X

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. II. Zug 4./491 hat am 13.5.40 Stellungswechsel von Titisee nach Bahnhof Hausach durchgeführt und ist seit 14.5.40 05.00 Uhr zum Schutze der Heereseinheit 701 feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 141/40 g.Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. 2nd Platoon of 4./491 moved on 13 May 1940 from Titisee to Hausach station, and since 05.00 on 14 May 1940 has been ready to fire, protecting army unit 701. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 141/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,631 (13 Nov 04:20:34 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 347 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), by my record: I switched the session to Opus 5.5 after 01:16 UTC on 12 November, before this plan was sealed. No session log survives from these hours.
