## 2 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1112–1114

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts, headed 170 (frame 1113) and 98 (frame 1114), one part per frame. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1112 (SRO 08, accepted 10.55, passed on 11.15 and 11.35; Briefbuch-Nr. F 96/40 g.Kdos.).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (19)  12 6 15 3 9 18 2 10 5 13 19 16 7 1 14 17 4 11 8
B (24)  7 13 18 9 22 5 17 21 8 19 2 23 11 15 3 24 12 16 1 20 6 14 10 4
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 258 letters. Six letters on the sheets do not make their words: DREIZWNEINS, NUQ, IU for IN, SCHWARCWALD, FRITC and MEUNSEQS. The two z's of part 2 are both written as this writer's c; his z in part 1 is a capital-Z form.

**What it says.** After a change of position, 4./R 321 (Eisb.) has been ready to fire in its new position at Bad Krozingen since 05.00 on 2 May 1940. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; the copy's struck-out "(Zimber)" is not in the radio text. What is new is the key of the day. The 9 May message (in this thread) has the same battery back with the command, in a new position at Bad Krozingen.

**The sender's spellings.** GROSS RICHARD and GROSS FRITZ for the capitals R and F, the brackets as X KLAMMER EISB X KLAMMER, ZWO for 2, and GEH X KDOS where the copy reads "g.Kdos."

**Method.** This used design S2, as for 14 and 15 May: both parts searched together, the commander's name held back from the crib as the hold-out, key lengths 15–30 and a stage-1 margin of 1.10.
- The copy has no part-break stroke. Before the seal, the letter counts of the two parts placed the break at the end of the body, and the decryption confirms it.
- After the miss of 12 May, the prereg sealed a family of four cribs that differ only in X places, to be run in order, stopping at the first hit. The first found the key, though its crib still had four X's out of place.

**Prereg.** Hash `a5a618d170fac76357abb63cd5ee97e5ab7c80f33c7cb8e3a5c4b3f51cd69cec` (13 Nov 2026 03:44:37 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 373 against a decoy ceiling of 262 (1.42 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.849, with 10 of 12 words.
- The held-back name decrypts at 0.933 against 0.333 for the best of 200 random key pairs. FLUGABW reads letter for letter; WARZWALD does not, because the sheet has c for its z.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 192 letters, against 20. Frame 1113 alone gives 131 against 15, and frame 1114 gives 61 against 10.

**Reading.** None of the 258 letters first read is judged a misreading. One of the six slips (the u of IU) has only a short stroke over it and may be a misreading of an n.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** Dudout's README in this target records a first attempt on frames 1113–1114, this message, which found no key. Nothing else found at 03:43 UTC on 13 Nov 2026.

#### The message

*Radio text, with word division*

*German*
4./R 321 (Eisb.) nach Stellungswechsel in neuer Stellung Bad Krozingen seit 2.5.1940 05.00 Uhr feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c Nr. F 96/40 geh. Kdos.

*English*
After a change of position, 4./R 321 (railway) has been ready to fire in its new position at Bad Krozingen since 05.00 on 2 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 96/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 258 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 6 places where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
