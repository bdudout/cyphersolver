## 8 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1084–1085

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts, one per radio form: headed 196 (frame 1084) and 178 (frame 1085). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1083 (SRO 073, accepted 16.20, passed on 17.30; letter-book number by hand). One hand-drawn stroke on it marks the part break.

**Both parts begin with an indicator group** (rlghs, ekeju), unlike 10 and 11 May. Both forms are in one hand.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (23)  19 15 23 8 20 12 3 18 10 6 17 21 2 11 7 16 4 22 1 14 9 5 13
B (27)  25 6 10 22 16 3 20 8 24 27 12 18 9 21 2 13 17 26 23 4 15 1 19 5 11 14 7
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 364 letters. One letter on the sheets does not make its word: VIERNUWL, a w where the L of VIERNULL is wanted.

**What it says.** I. Zug 1./861 moved on 8 May 1940 from Schiftung to the Lauerstellung west of Hügelsheim, and has been ready to fire since 05.15, at grid R 343280 H 540670. It agrees with the teleprinter copy, letter-book number F 122/40 included.

**The sender's spellings.**
- The heading is GEH X KDOS, at both ends of the message; on the days read before, GEH X KDOS only closes messages (2 and 15 May).
- ROEM X EINS X ZUG X EINS X STRIQ X AQTSEQSEINS; the year as EINSNEUNVIERNULL, as on 9 May; SCHIFTUNG and SCHWARZWALD with CH.
- The grid letters as GROSSRIQARD and GROSSHEINZ; 2 as ZWO; the signature FLUGABWKDO X SCHWARZWALD X ROEM X EINS X CAESAR NR GROSSFRITZ, with no X between CAESAR, NR and GROSSFRITZ.

**Method.** The same search (key lengths 15–30, stage-1 margin 1.10), in the design for one short message: both parts searched, the commander's name held back from the crib as the hold-out.
- The letter counts, fitted with and without each part's first group, set the parse before the seal: an indicator in both parts.
- Their first run left E and H over in part 1. GEH X KDOS was then offered as the heading, marked as without precedent there; with it part 1 fits to two letters, one of them a letter listed as doubtful beforehand. Three crib members that differ only in X places were sealed as a family; the first found the key.

**Prereg.** Hash `502d84c2cedd33253cec872099ec88fb305e160f749db16f847ad0119bb02b7c` (13 Nov 2026 14:08:59 UTC), timestamped at 14:09:01–02 UTC by the same three authorities, before the run began at 14:09:13. The blind reading of the sheets is timestamped at 13:54:13, before the teleprinter copy was read.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 571 against a decoy ceiling of 401 (1.42 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.909, with 11 of 12 words.
- The held-back name decrypts at 0.864 against 0.409 for the best of 200 random key pairs, with both FLUGABW and WARZWALD.
- A second decoder agrees. On 30 common German trigrams the keys score 20, against 11 for the best of 1,000 random key pairs.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 248 letters, against 23.

**Reading.** 2 of 364 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, both on the list of doubtful letters made beforehand, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 13:42 UTC on 13 Nov 2026. Dudout's list of first attempts does not include these frames.

#### The message

*Radio text, with word division*
GEH X KDOS X ROEM X EINS X ZUG X EINS X STRIQ X AQTSEQSEINS X HAT AM X AQT X FUENF X EINSNEUNVIERNULL X STELLUNGSWEQSEL DURQGEFUEHRT VON X SCHIFTUNG X [I/R]N DIE LAUERSTELLUNG WESTLIQ X HUEGELSHEIM X UND IST SEIT X NULLFUENFEINSFUENF X UHR ‖ FEUERBEREIT X G[R/E]OSSRIQARD X DREIVIERDREIZWOAQTNULL X GROSSHEINZ X FUENFVIERNULLSEQSSIEBENNULL X FLUGABWKDO X SCHWARZWALD X ROEM X EINS X CAESAR NR GROSSFRITZ X EINSZWOZWO X STRIQ X VIERNU[L/W]L X GEH X KDOS X

*German*
Geh. Kdos. I. Zug 1./861 hat am 8.5.1940 Stellungswechsel durchgeführt von Schiftung in die Lauerstellung westlich Hügelsheim und ist seit 05.15 Uhr feuerbereit, R 343280 H 540670. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 122/40 g. Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. On 8 May 1940, 1st platoon of 1./861 changed position from Schiftung to the waiting position west of Hügelsheim, and has been ready to fire since 05.15, grid R 343280 H 540670. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 122/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 364 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
