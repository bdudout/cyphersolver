**What they say.** Message 1 reports three changes of position (1./251 from Balzhofen to north of Memprechtshofen; IV. Zug 2./942 from Thiengen to near the Lonza works at Waldshut; III. Zug 3./942 from the Efringen–Egringen crossroads to west of Säckingen) with the times they were ready to fire. Message 2 adds three reference points to the numbering of Flak group Schwarzwald-Süd: 48 Mönchenstein, 49 Zeiningen, 50 Laufenburg (Switzerland). Both agree with the teleprinter copies on frames 0925 and 0932, so no unknown text was read; what is new is the key of the day.

**Spellings not on the two days read before:** capitals announced as GROSZ (GROSZ RICHARD, GROSZ HEINZ, GROSZ FRITZ), the hyphen sent as STRIQ, the colon as DOPPELPUNKT (once DOPPUNKT), SSO as SIEGFRIED SIEGFRIED OTTO.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (interlinear, margin, clear passages, same file): the clear teleprinter copy of message 1 on frame 0925.

1. The cipher text was read from the NARA frame images before any decryption, and the reading was hashed.
2. The clear text of frame 0925 was turned into radio spelling by fixed rules taken from the two days already read (866 letters against 849 on the forms).
3. Search from the second key's side, as in Harumi-Matsuba's method, in code the model wrote: for every pair of key lengths from 8 to 35, the second key was annealed so that, with the second transposition undone, neighbouring letters are crib letters standing "length of the first key" apart. Message 1 only. The pair (26, 22) scored 1,321; the next pair 865; four decoys with the letters of each part shuffled reached 787 at most.
4. With the second key fixed, the first key was annealed on three- and four-letter strings of the crib.
5. Message 2, which had not been used, was then decrypted with those keys as they stood.

**Sealed plan.** The hypotheses, tests, pass marks, decoys and forecasts were written down and hashed before the run: SHA-256 `522d5c5c77516267e7b486cf0448169442c7504842a4bcc27e21b2c08061cf86`, sealed 11 Nov 2026 22:59:44 UTC; the run went from 23:00:00 to 23:06:38 UTC. The plan's file is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,496 (12 Nov 2026 00:17:34 UTC, the block's own time). The Bitcoin anchors given with each day prove only that the plans existed by the block's time, which is after each run; that each plan was sealed before its run rests on its RFC 3161 timestamps where given, and otherwise on the runs' output files and file times. Every plan sealed from 13 Nov 05:18 UTC on (24, 26, 11, 10, 8 and 6 May and 9 April) also carries RFC 3161 timestamps from FreeTSA, DigiCert and Sectigo, given with its day.
