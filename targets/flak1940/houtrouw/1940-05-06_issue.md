## 6 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1091–1092

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts: part 1 on a radio form headed 183 (frame 1091), part 2 on squared paper headed 176 (frame 1092), though the sheet holds 171 letters. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1090 (SRO 055, accepted 16.45, passed on 17.30; letter-book number by hand). Two hand-drawn strokes on it mark the part break.

**Part 1 begins with an indicator group; part 2 does not**, as on 10 and 11 May. Both sheets are in one hand.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (18)  9 2 11 7 14 4 18 12 6 1 15 8 3 17 13 5 10 16
B (22)  18 5 16 12 7 20 14 3 19 9 4 15 22 8 1 17 6 11 2 21 13 10
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 349 letters. Part 2 is complete as it stands; its heading's 176 is a miscount. One letter on the sheets does not make its word: SIEHENNULLEINS, an h written for the b of SIEBEN.

**What it says.** II. Zug 4./491 changed position on 5 May 1940 at 03.10, and has been ready to fire since 07.00 that day in its new position at Titisee station, protecting unit 701. The 14 May message (in this thread) has the same platoon moving on from Titisee to Hausach station. The radio text agrees with the teleprinter copy, but leaves out ROEM in the signature and the year of the letter-book number (F EINSEINSSEQS STRIQ GKDOS).

**The sender's spellings.**
- X GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE X ROEM ZWEI X ZUG X VIER STRIQ VIERNEUNEINS; both dates with EINSNEUNVIERNULL.
- Part 2 has few separators: FEUERBEREIT FLUGABWEHRKOMMANDO SQWARZWALD EINS CAESAR NR X FRITZ.

**Method.** The same search (key lengths 15–30, stage-1 margin 1.10), both parts searched. Because part 2 seemed to lack five letters at an unknown place, the heading of part 1 was held back from the crib as the hold-out, in place of the commander's name in part 2. Two framings of part 2 were sealed in order: five unknown letters after its end, then the sheet as it stands. The first missed (stage 1 at 1.00, though with the true lengths on top); the second found the key.

**Prereg.** Hash `ac5e3109d7731a21545a05babd139af44cc6fd4688208fbe1797f2fbc768b0dc` (13 Nov 2026 14:35:09 UTC), timestamped at 14:35:09–10 UTC by the same three authorities, before the runs began at 14:35:19. The blind reading of the sheets is timestamped at 14:27:36, before the teleprinter copy was read.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 563 against a decoy ceiling of 414 (1.36 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.872, with 9 of 12 words.
- The held-back heading decrypts letter for letter (1.0) against 0.421 for the best of 200 random key pairs.
- A second decoder agrees. On 30 common German trigrams the keys score 20, against 12 for the best of 1,000 random key pairs.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 252 letters, against 24.

**Reading.** 5 of 349 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked; three of them are this hand's n.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 14:24 UTC on 13 Nov 2026. Dudout's list of first attempts does not include these frames.

#### The message

*Radio text, with word division*
X GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE X ROEM ZWEI X ZUG X VIER STRIQ VIERNEUNEI[N/H]S X HAT AM X FUENF X FUENF X EINSNEUNVIER[N/W]ULL X NULLDREIEIN[S/D][N/W]ULL X UHR STELLUNGSWEQSEL DURQGEFUEHRT UND IST SEIT X FUENF X FUENF X EINSNEUNVIERNULL X ‖ NULLSIEBENNULLNULL X UHR IN NEUER STELLUNG BEI BAHNHOF X TITISEE X ZUM SQUTZE DER EINHEIT X SIE[B/H]ENNULLEINS X FEUERBEREIT FLUGA[B/K]WEHRKOMMANDO SQWARZWALD EINS CAESAR NR X FRITZ EINSEINSSEQS STRIQ GKDOS X

*German*
Geh. Kdos. II. Zug 4./491 hat am 5.5.1940 03.10 Uhr Stellungswechsel durchgeführt und ist seit 5.5.1940 07.00 Uhr in neuer Stellung bei Bahnhof Titisee zum Schutze der Einheit 701 feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 116 g. Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. 2nd platoon of 4./491 changed position on 5 May 1940 at 03.10, and since 07.00 on 5 May 1940 it has been ready to fire in its new position at Titisee station, protecting unit 701. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 116, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 349 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
