## 10 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1069–1071

**Documents covered.** One message in three parts, one per radio form: headed 188 (frame 1069), 172 (frame 1070) and 117 (frame 1071). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1068 (SRO 089, accepted 09.55, passed on 11.54 and 12.10; letter-book number by hand). Two hand-drawn strokes on it mark the part breaks.

**Part 1 begins with an indicator group; parts 2–3 do not**, as on 11 May. All three forms are in one hand.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (29)  27 11 16 1 24 8 21 4 28 12 25 3 18 15 23 2 26 17 5 9 13 20 29 6 19 10 22 7 14
B (26)  10 17 21 13 3 19 8 14 1 9 16 26 22 6 20 11 5 24 18 15 2 23 7 12 4 25
```

**Result.** All three parts read: 472 letters. One letter on the sheets does not make its word: FLNGABWEHR, a u written without its breve.

**What it says.** 1./491 moved on 9 May from Sasbach to Hecklingen, and has been ready to fire since 07.45 on 10 May. Two more 60 cm searchlights, from the Flak stores depot at Deisslingen, are ready at the E.-Hafen Eutingen position, making 8 there; the 9 May message (in this thread) put 4 + 2 there. It agrees with the teleprinter copy except in the letter-book number: the radio text has 129 (EINSZWEINEUN) where the first reading of the copy had 124.

**The sender's spellings.**
- The dots of both dates are sent as X PUNKT X (NEUN X PUNKT X FUENF X PUNKT X VIERNULL), on no other day read.
- EINS X STRIQ X VIERNEUNEINS; SASBACH with CH and HECKLINGEN with CK; E STRIQ HAFEN X EUTINGEN; SEQSNULL X CM.
- FLUGABWEHRKOMMANDO SCHWARZWALD with no X between; GKDOS at the end.

**Method.** The same search (key lengths 15–30, stage-1 margin 1.10), with parts 1–2 searched and part 3 held out.
- The letter counts, fitted with and without each part's first group, set the parse before the seal: an indicator in part 1 only.
- They also asked for four PUNKTs in part 1, which then fit every letter exactly. Four crib members that differ in one X were sealed as a family; the first found the key.

**Prereg.** Hash `61fedb8a596842f332df3c137e5d1edc36b18ec54836a73e6717b42aa2f78239` (13 Nov 2026 07:36:32 UTC), timestamped at 07:36:32–33 UTC by the same three authorities, before the run began at 07:37:20. The blind reading of the sheets is timestamped at 07:15:59, before the teleprinter copy was read.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 601 against a decoy ceiling of 414 (1.45 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.963, with 12 of 12 words.
- Part 3, held out, decrypts at 0.923 against 0.222 for the best of 200 random key pairs, with 10 of 12 words.
- A second decoder agrees. On 30 common German trigrams the keys score 31, against 15 for the best of 1,000 random key pairs.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 379 letters, against 33.

**Reading.** 4 of 472 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked; two of them lie under later heavy marks.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 07:08 UTC on 13 Nov 2026. Dudout's list of first attempts does not include these frames.

#### The message

*Radio text, with word division*
GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE X EINS X STRIQ X VIERNEUNEINS X HAT AM X NEUN X PUNKT X FUENF X PUNKT X VIERNULL X STELLUNGSWEQSEL VON X SASBACH X NAQ X HECKLINGEN X DURQGEFUEHRT UND IST SEIT X EINSNULL X PUNKT X FUENF X PUNKT X VIERNULL X ‖ X [N/W]ULLSIEBENVIERFUENF X UHR FEUERBEREIT X IN STELLUNG E STRIQ HAFEN X EUTINGEN X SIND ZUSAETZLIQ WEITERE X ZWEI X SQEINWERFER X SEQSNULL X CM X VOM FLAK BESTAENDELAGER X DEISSLI[N/M]GEN X KOMMEND X LEUQTBEREIT X ‖ SOMIT SIND DORT X A[Q/G]T X WERFER LEU[Q/G]TBEREIT X FL[U/N]GABWEHRKOMMANDO SCHWARZWALD X ROEM X EINS X C X NR X F X EINSZWEINEUN X STRIQ X VIERNULL X GKDOS X

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. 1./491 hat am 9.5.40 Stellungswechsel von Sasbach nach Hecklingen durchgeführt und ist seit 10.5.40 07.45 Uhr feuerbereit. In Stellung E.-Hafen Eutingen sind zusätzlich weitere 2 Scheinwerfer 60 cm, vom Flak-Beständelager Deisslingen kommend, leuchtbereit. Somit sind dort 8 Werfer leuchtbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 129/40 g. Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. On 9 May 1940, 1./491 moved from Sasbach to Hecklingen, and has been ready to fire since 07.45 on 10 May 1940. At the E.-Hafen Eutingen position, two more 60 cm searchlights, come from the Flak stores depot at Deisslingen, are ready to illuminate. There are thus 8 searchlights ready there. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 129/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 472 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
