## 24 May 1940: key of the day · frames 919–923

**Documents covered.** One message in three parts, headed 239 (frame 920, continued on frame 921), 203 (frame 922) and 89 (frame 923). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 919 (accepted 11.40, passed on 13.20; Briefbuch-Nr. F 193/40 g.Kdos.), with hand-drawn part-break strokes.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (25)  24 13 6 19 16 9 23 14 3 10 22 4 12 7 15 1 20 5 17 8 25 2 11 18 21
B (29)  11 27 14 6 19 22 29 10 23 4 15 28 9 17 20 5 12 26 1 18 7 13 21 2 24 8 16 3 25
```

**Result.** All three parts read: 513 letters, plus 3 lost. Part 2 is headed 203, but the sheet holds 200 and no continuation is filmed. The three letters fall one each in STRIQ, DREI and DREINULL. One letter on the sheets does not make its word: NULL with an m for its n.

**What it says.** 3./251 moved on 23 May from Elgersweier to target square 842865 b (south of Ichenheim), and has been ready to fire since 02.00 on 24 May. III./3./942 and IV./2./942 returned to their old positions on 23 May, ready from 20.30 and 16.00. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day. It follows the 23 May message (in this thread), which moved the same two platoons of 942 on 22 May.

**The sender's spellings.** GKDOS at the start and at the end. BRUNO for the b of the target square (on 23 May this office wrote BERTA). STELLUNGWEQSEL, SUDL, ZURUEQGEKEHRT. The commander's name as FLUGABW X KDO, and NRO.

**Method.** The same search was run on parts 1–2, with part 3 held out; key lengths 15–30 and a stage-1 margin of 1.10.
- The crib was cut at the strokes and fitted to the letter counts before the seal. The fit was weak (total L1 28 of 516).
- A family of four runs was sealed: two bracket layouts, and a fallback that searched parts 1 and 3 instead. The first run found the key.

**Prereg.** Hash `67b890897240403108fcfd616793bdbd391044e7b85d500827135bd3ccaba7f2` (13 Nov 2026 05:18:43 UTC). It is timestamped at 05:18:44 UTC by three RFC 3161 authorities (FreeTSA, DigiCert, Sectigo), before the run began at 05:19:16. The blind reading of the sheets is timestamped at 05:05:20, before the teleprinter copy was read.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 625 against a decoy ceiling of 500 (1.25 times).
- Parts 1–2 agreed at 0.952, with 9 of 12 words.
- Held-out part 3 agreed at 0.843, with 10 of 12 words; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.258.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 367 letters, against 29.

**Reading.** 2 of 513 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 05:17 UTC on 13 Nov 2026. These frames are not among Dudout's first attempts.

*English*
Secret command matter. On 23 May 1940, 3./251 moved from Elgersweier to target square 842865 b (south of Ichenheim); it has been ready to fire since 02.00 on 24 May 1940. III./3./942 and IV./2./942 returned to their old positions on 23 May 1940 and have been ready to fire since 20.30 and 16.00. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 193/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 513 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word; the 3 lost letters are supplied from the words.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
