# 16 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 1028, SRO 0160, accepted 16 May 1940 12.15, passed on 15.48 and 16.25)

Secret command matter. On 15 May 1940 two 2-cm platoons from Flak Group Friedrichshafen were assigned to Flak Command Black Forest:
- 2nd Platoon of 2./932, ready to fire in map square 241454 b since 19.30 on 15 May;
- 3rd Platoon of 2./932, ready in map square 251964 b since 19.30 on 15 May.
