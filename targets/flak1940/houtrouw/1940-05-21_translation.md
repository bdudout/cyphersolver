# 21 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 0963, SRO 0202, accepted 21 May 1940 16.30, passed on 18.30 and 18.50)

II/1./942 changed position on 15 May 1940 from Tumringen to target square 256434 a/c (Hagen). IV/3./942 changed position on 19 May 1940 from Istein to target square 245420 a/b (Fischingen) and has been ready to fire since 01.00 on 20 May. III/3./861 changed position on 20 May 1940 from Rastatt-North to target square 627122 d (Plittersdorf) and has been ready to fire since 22.15 on 20 May 1940. I/3./861 changed position on 20 May 1940 from Rastatt-South to target square 626633 d (Rastatt-North) and has been ready to fire since 22.15. All values are plotted on grid strip 2. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 183/40. Secret command matter.
