# 23 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### Message 1 (teleprinter copy: frame 0925, SRO 0225, 23 May 1940, accepted 11.40, passed on 14.05 and 14.50)

1./251 changed position on 21 May 1940 from Balzhofen to target square 696445 b (north of Memprechtshofen) and has been ready to fire since 03.07 on 22 May 1940. IV Platoon 2./942 changed position on 22 May 1940 from Thiengen to 1 km north-east of the Lonza works (Waldshut) and has been ready to fire since 04.30 on 22 May 1940. III Platoon 3./942 changed position on 22 May 1940 from the Efringen–Egringen crossroads to 2 km west of Säckingen, map reference R 264540 H 527070, and has been ready to fire since 04.30. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 186/40.

#### Message 2 (teleprinter copy: frame 0932, SRO 0227, 23 May 1940, accepted 16.15, passed on 17.00 and 17.10)

Secret command matter. Subject: new reference points. Flak Group Black Forest South had been given the numbers 11 to 47 as numbers for reference points for the target-square grid. Because the High Rhine upstream from Basel was left out, there being no Flak positions there, the following are now added in view of the present occupation: No. 48 Mönchenstein, SSE of Basel; No. 49 Zeiningen, SSE of Rheinfelden; No. 50 Laufenburg (Switzerland). Flak Command Black Forest, I c, No. F 190/40, secret command matter.
