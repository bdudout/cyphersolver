## 12 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1055–1057

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts, headed 173 (frame 1056) and 200 (frame 1057, squared paper), one part per frame. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1055 (SRO 0109, accepted 10.10, passed on 11.45 and 12.00; Briefbuch-Nr. F 135/40 g.K.). It is the only message of the day on the reel.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (24)  17 9 22 6 12 18 23 3 14 20 7 15 2 19 10 21 4 16 11 24 1 13 5 8
B (28)  25 22 15 9 21 5 12 17 6 23 2 18 7 11 24 28 14 20 26 3 8 16 1 13 10 4 19 27
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 363 letters. Three letters on the sheets do not make their words: KOMMANSOSAQE and SURQGEFUEHRT, where this writer's s stands for d, and X where CAESAR wants its C.

**What it says.** On 9 May, I. Zug 2./851 moved from the Altenheimer mill to Planzeiger R 341120 H 537430. It has been ready to fire there since 04.00 that day. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day. The 9 May message (in this thread) has the same platoon reach the mill at 02.15 that morning.

**The sender's spellings.** RIQARD and HEINZ for the R and H of the reference. The typed hyphen of Altenheimer-Mühle is sent as STRIQ. STRIQ never has an X on either side, and each part opens and closes with an X.

**Method, and a first miss.**
- **The first sealed attempt missed.** It used the short-message design S2, with the crib fitted to the letter counts. Stage 1 gave 1.08 times the decoy ceiling against a bar of 1.10, on the first run and on the one allowed fallback (prereg hash `5b85be969bc34119ee843bb5b8876486cce363039f87c6fff99930c8cbe7a00a`, 13 Nov 2026 02:32:27 UTC).
- **The cause.** The crib carried six X faults (X's missing or extra in the wrong places). In rehearsals on solved days, moving a single X to a wrong place sank the true key in three of four trials on 15 May, and less often on longer messages, even with the count of X's kept right.
- **The second sealed attempt** added one spelling with precedent: STRIQ with no X at the hyphen. It ran a family of four cribs, differing only in X places, in a fixed order sealed in advance, and stopped at the first hit (prereg hash `2a74f89966a4967a787753e3a837c93660cc6f9909979f96c203cd94413c7ecf`, 13 Nov 2026 02:50:12 UTC).
  - Run 1 gave a stage-1 candidate that failed both later bars.
  - Run 2 gave none.
  - Run 3 found the key.

**Checks (run 3).**
- Stage 1 scored 485 against a decoy ceiling of 412 (1.18 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.857, with 11 of 12 words.
- The held-back name decrypts at 0.967 against 0.333 for the best of 200 random key pairs; FLUGABW and WARZWALD both read letter for letter.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 284 letters, against 25. Frame 1056 alone gives 125 against 15.
- The frame-1057 figure is not independent: NARA's page text for that frame had been in the model's working context before the frame was read.

**Reading.** 1 of 363 letters first read was a misreading in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** Dudout's README in this target records a first attempt on frames 1056–1057, this message, which found no key. Nothing else found at 02:49 UTC on 13 Nov 2026.

*Radio text, with word division*
X GEHEIME KOMMAN[D/S]OSAQE X ROEM EINS X ZUG X ZWEI STRIQ AQTFUENFEINS X HAT AM X NEUN X FUENF X VIERNULL STELLUNGSWEQSEL VON X ALTENHEIMER STRIQ MUEHLE X NAQ PLANZEIGER X RIQARD DREIVIEREINSEINSZWEINULL X ‖ X HEINZ FUENFDREISIEBENVIERDREINULL X [D/S]URQGEFUEHRT UND IST SEIT X NEUN X FUENF X VIERNULL X NULLVIERNULLNULL X UHR FEUERBEREIT X FLUGABWEHRKOMMANDO SCHWARZWALD X ROEM EINS [C/X]AESAR X NR X FRITZ EI[N/M]SDREIFUENF STRIQ VIERNULL X GKDOS X

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. I. Zug 2./851 hat am 9.5.40 Stellungswechsel von Altenheimer-Mühle nach Planzeiger R 341120 H 537430 durchgeführt und ist seit 9.5.40 04.00 Uhr feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 135/40 g.Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. On 9 May 1940, 1st Platoon of 2./851 moved from the Altenheimer mill to map reference R 341120 H 537430. It has been ready to fire there since 04.00 on 9 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 135/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,631 (13 Nov 04:20:34 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 363 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 3 places where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
