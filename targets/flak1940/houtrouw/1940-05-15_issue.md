## 15 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1038–1040

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts, headed 192 (frame 1040) and 134 (frame 1039); the film has the two sheets in reverse order. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1038 (SRO 0139, accepted 08.55, passed on 10.15; Briefbuch-Nr. F 151/40 g. Kdos). It is the only message of the day on the reel.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (29)  26 9 15 23 7 16 20 4 12 27 18 5 28 13 24 2 19 6 25 11 22 1 14 8 29 21 17 3 10
B (26)  20 15 11 4 17 6 23 12 26 19 7 14 1 18 9 22 2 24 13 5 25 10 3 21 8 16
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 316 letters. One letter on the sheets does not make its word: FRUERBEREIT.

**What it says.** 1./35 moved on 14 May from Kuppenheim to north of Rastatt, and has been ready to fire there since 04.40 on 15 May. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day. The 16 May message (in this thread) reports the unit back at Kuppenheim that evening.

**The sender's spellings.** GROSS FRIEDRIQ for the capital F of the number (every other day has FRITZ), and GEH X KDOS where the copy reads "g. Kdos."

**Method.** This used the short-message design of 14 May (design S2). Both parts were searched together, and the commander's name in the signature was held back from the crib as the hold-out; key lengths were 15–30 and the stage-1 margin 1.10. The copy has no part-break stroke, so the crib was not cut. The spellings were fitted to the letter counts of the whole message before the seal (L1 10 of 316). Three of the fitted spellings were wrong, and the key was found anyway.

**Prereg.** Hash `503b049c0d78c943899b5be0c6ede4e0d464f3e41afd1456037b515129bf160a` (13 Nov 2026 02:00:27 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 486 against a decoy ceiling of 347 (1.40 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.854, with 10 of 12 words.
- The held-back name decrypts at 0.778 against 0.333 for the best of 200 random key pairs; FLUGABW and WARZWALD both read letter for letter. The held text carried a hyphen the sender did not write, which is why the match is lower than on 14 May (0.966).
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 230 letters, against 21.

**Reading.** 1 of 316 letters first read was a misreading in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** Dudout's README in this target records a first attempt on frames 1039–1040, this message, which found no key. Nothing else found at 01:52 UTC on 13 Nov 2026.

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 1038, SRO 0139, accepted 15 May 1940 08.55, passed on 10.15)

*Radio text, with word division*
X GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE X EINS X STRIQ X DREIFUENF X HAT AM X EINSVIER X FUENF X VIERNULL X STELLUNGSWEQSEL VON X KUPPENHEIM X NAQ NOERD[L/R]IQ X RASTATT X GEMAQT UND IST SEIT X NULLVIERVIERNULL X UHR AM X EINSFUENF X FUENF X VIERNULL X ‖ IN NEUER STELLUNG F[E/R]UERBEREIT X FLUGABWEHRKOMMANDO X SCHWARZWALD X ROEM X EINS X CAESAR X NR X GROSS FRIEDRIQ X EINSFUENFEINS X STRIQ X VIERNULL X GEH X KDOS

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. 1./35 hat am 14.5.40 Stellungswechsel von Kuppenheim nach nördlich Rastatt gemacht und ist seit 04.40 Uhr am 15.5.40 in neuer Stellung feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 151/40 geh. Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. 1./35 moved on 14 May 1940 from Kuppenheim to north of Rastatt, and has been ready to fire in its new position since 04.40 on 15 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 151/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,631 (13 Nov 04:20:34 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 316 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), by my record: I switched the session to Opus 5.5 after 01:16 UTC on 12 November, before this plan was sealed. No session log survives from these hours.
