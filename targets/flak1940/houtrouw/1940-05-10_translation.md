# 10 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message

Secret command matter. On 9 May 1940, 1./491 moved from Sasbach to Hecklingen, and has been ready to fire since 07.45 on 10 May 1940. At the E.-Hafen Eutingen position, two more 60 cm searchlights, come from the Flak stores depot at Deisslingen, are ready to illuminate. There are thus 8 searchlights ready there. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 129/40, secret command matter.
