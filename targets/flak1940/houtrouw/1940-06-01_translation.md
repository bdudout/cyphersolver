# 1 June 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 0888, SRO 05, accepted 1 June 1940 13.15, passed on 14.30 and 14.50)

Secret command matter. To protect army unloadings, the following moved into position: I/2./942 from the Schluchsee dam to the Donaueschingen road bridge, ready to fire since 22.49 on 31 May 1940; II/2./942 from Schwarzabruck to Immendingen station, ready since 22.49 on 31 May; I/Light Reserve Flak Battery 453 from Forbach to Horb station, ready since 22.20 on 31 May; 4./321 (Eis) from Offenburg to the Hüfingen sawmill, ready since 03.20 on 1 June; II/4./251 from Oberkirch to the viaduct on the Hattingen–Tuttlingen railway, ready since 10.00 on 1 June. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, 212/40, secret command matter.
