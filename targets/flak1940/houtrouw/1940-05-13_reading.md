# 13 May 1940: reading (radio text with word division, and German), by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

Notation (the contributor's): In the radio text, [x/y] means the word wants x and the first reading had y. Brackets mark the sheet's own slips and some of the letters corrected after the key was known; the lists above give all of both. A letter in brackets alone was lost from the film and is supplied from the words; ‖ marks a part break; X is the sender's separator.

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. I. Zug 9./351 hat am 12.5.40 Stellungswechsel durchgeführt von Thiengen nach Rottweil und ist seit 12.5.40 21.45 Uhr feuerbereit. IV.
