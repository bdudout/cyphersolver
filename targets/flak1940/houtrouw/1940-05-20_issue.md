## 20 May 1940: key of the day · frames 0977–1003

**Documents covered.** Two messages, both from Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c:
- **Message A**: frames 0978–0985, five parts headed 205, 203, 199, 207 and 109. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 0977 (SRO 0193).
- **Message B**: frames 0997–1003, six parts headed 181, 186, 185, 172, 210 and 152. The clear copy is frame 0996 (SRO 0189).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (27)  12 27 24 7 19 14 5 22 10 17 2 8 16 25 9 4 13 20 26 1 21 6 15 18 3 11 23
B (23)  19 11 5 14 23 17 12 6 18 3 22 13 8 1 15 7 20 2 10 21 4 16 9
```

**Result.** All eleven parts read: 1,954 letters. Seven letters on the sheets do not make their words (FIER, NULN, SUEDOETWAERTS, NWLL, ZVO, and two ZWO with a v-like o).

**Method.** The same search was run on message A alone, with message B held out. The crib was cut at the hand-drawn part breaks on the teleprinter copies and fitted to the letter counts before the seal.

**Prereg.** Hash `f6bea0e322a74770fa68cdf3d5419ce781f30f9749beb531ca530b198b578703` (12 Nov 2026 21:14:55 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 1,552 against a decoy ceiling of 940.
- Message A agreed at 0.972.
- Hold-out B agreed at 0.949, with 10 of 12 words; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.205.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 1,344 letters, against 82.

**Reading.** 26 of 1,954 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** Dudout's README in this target (`targets/flak1940/dudout/README.md`) records a first attempt on frames 978–985, which is message A; it found no key. Nothing else found at 21:25 UTC on 12 Nov 2026.
