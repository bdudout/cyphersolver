## 1 June 1940: key of the day · frames 0888–0894

**Documents covered.** One message in six parts, headed 184, 168, 177, 156, 164 and 84, one part per frame. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 0888 (SRO 05).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (21)  14 3 11 8 21 5 16 2 18 13 7 10 1 19 6 17 12 20 4 15 9
B (26)  20 14 23 16 3 11 24 7 26 18 10 22 13 1 17 21 5 12 8 19 2 15 25 6 9 4
```

**Result.** All six parts read: 903 letters. Sixteen letters on the sheets do not make their words, thirteen of them in the first hand (among them TREIEINS twice for DREIEINS); one more is unclear.

**The sender's spellings.** DOPPUNKT, STRASZENBRUEQE, BINDESTRIQ and SQWARZWALD.

**Method.** The same search was run on parts 1–4 alone, with parts 5 and 6 held out. The crib was cut at the hand-drawn part breaks and fitted to the letter counts before the seal.

**Prereg.** Hash `738ac131fe6c40f8a037169ff7040e50f36f39a402b6e722303ee75a371a94f3` (12 Nov 2026 22:20:03 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 1,062 against a decoy ceiling of 680.
- Parts 1–4 agreed at 0.928.
- Held-out parts 5–6 agreed at 0.870; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.210.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 604 letters, against 45.

**Reading.** 13 of 903 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** Dudout's README in this target records a first attempt on frames 889–894, this message, which found no key; it names inexact cribs from NARA's OCR as the cause. Nothing else found at 22:17 UTC on 12 Nov 2026. The ledger's 4 June message moves four of the same units on.

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. Zum Schutze von Heeresausladungen gingen in Stellung: I./2./942 von Staumauer Schluchsee nach Donaueschingen Straßenbrücke und ist seit 31.5.40 22.49 Uhr feuerbereit. *English*
Secret command matter. To protect army unloadings, the following moved into position: I/2./942 from the Schluchsee dam to the Donaueschingen road bridge, ready to fire since 22.49 on 31 May 1940; II/2./942 from Schwarzabruck to Immendingen station, ready since 22.49 on 31 May; I/Light Reserve Flak Battery 453 from Forbach to Horb station, ready since 22.20 on 31 May; 4./321 (Eis) from Offenburg to the Hüfingen sawmill, ready since 03.20 on 1 June; II/4./251 from Oberkirch to the viaduct on the Hattingen–Tuttlingen railway, ready since 10.00 on 1 June. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, 212/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,619 (13 Nov 02:29:54 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 903 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 17 places where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.
