## 16 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1028–1035

**Documents covered.** One message in seven parts, headed 190, 191, 181, 173, 182, 183 and 94, one part per frame. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1028 (SRO 0160, accepted 12.15, passed on 15.48 and 16.25; Briefbuch-Nr. F 160/40 geh. Kdos).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (20)  17 4 13 7 15 10 2 19 5 8 11 1 16 20 3 12 9 18 6 14
B (25)  14 19 6 13 4 23 10 7 21 15 2 18 22 8 11 16 25 12 1 20 5 17 9 3 24
```

**Result.** All seven parts read: 1,159 letters. Two letters on the sheets do not make their words: UUR for UHR, and KIPPENHEIM where the copy has Kuppenheim (both are towns in Baden; a slip or a correction cannot be told apart from these sheets).

**What it says.** On 15 May II. and III. Zug 2./932 (2 cm) were assigned from Flakgruppe Friedrichshafen and placed under Light Reserve Battalion 942. Two composite platoons were formed near Welmlingen and south of Rheinweiler. 1./35 F returned to its old permanent position. The teleprinter copy carries a hand edit ("Flakgruppe Süd" struck out, "lei. Res. Abt. 942" written above); the radio text has the edited version. Otherwise it agrees with the copy; what is new is the key of the day.

**Method.** The same search, with its own settings (key lengths 8–35, stage-1 margin 1.25), was run on parts 1–4 alone, with parts 5–7 held out. The crib was cut at the six hand-drawn part breaks and fitted to the letter counts before the seal (total L1 11 of 1,159 letters). "2 cm" was offered as CENTIMETER, the spelling Harumi-Matsuba read on 17 May; the counts chose ZENTIMETER.

**Prereg.** Hash `ca37b018a9b9e589294fef0f0525cd0e57fb81e801c0122fc78525bc7e667844` (13 Nov 2026 01:26:42 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 1,585 against a decoy ceiling of 800 (1.98 times).
- Parts 1–4 agreed at 0.986, with 12 of 12 words.
- Held-out parts 5–7 agreed at 0.996, with 11 of 12 words; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.225.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 785 letters, against 66.

**Reading.** 1 of 1,159 letters first read was a misreading in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 01:11 UTC on 13 Nov 2026. Dudout's list of first attempts does not include these frames. The ledger's 17 May message is a related report (I. Zug 2./932, assigned a day later).

*English*
Secret command matter. On 15 May 1940 two 2-cm platoons from Flak Group Friedrichshafen were assigned to Flak Command Black Forest:
- 2nd Platoon of 2./932, ready to fire in map square 241454 b since 19.30 on 15 May;
- 3rd Platoon of 2./932, ready in map square 251964 b since 19.30 on 15 May.

Both platoons are placed under Light Reserve Battalion 942.

A newly formed platoon, with one gun each from I./3./942 and II./3./942, has been ready to fire in position 8077 west of Welmlingen since 19.30. A second newly formed platoon, with one gun each from III./5./491 and IV./5./491, has been in position 2130 south of Rheinweiler since 21.45, ready to fire.

1./35 F returned on 15 May to its old permanent position near K[u/i]ppenheim and has been ready to fire since 22.50.

Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 160/40, secret command matter.
