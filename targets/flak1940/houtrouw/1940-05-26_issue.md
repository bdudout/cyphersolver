## 26 May 1940: key of the day · frames 916–918

**Documents covered.** One message in two parts, headed 212 (frame 917, continued on frame 918) and 81 (frame 918). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 916 (SRO 0260, accepted 10.50, passed on 12.20; Briefbuch-Nr. F 199/40 geh. Kdos.). It has no part-break stroke.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (27)  13 2 19 11 7 26 16 1 22 12 8 25 4 15 24 27 20 23 5 9 14 17 3 21 10 6 18
B (21)  8 20 12 1 15 21 7 10 4 18 14 3 11 6 17 2 13 9 16 5 19
```

**Result.** Both parts read: 283 letters. One letter on the sheets does not make its word: KARLSRUHE with a u that lacks its bar.

**What it says.** 4./321 (Eis) moved on 25 May from Karlsruhe to Offenburg, and has been ready to fire since 05.00 on 26 May. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day. The same battery is in the 2 May and 9 May messages (in this thread).

**The sender's spellings.** GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE at the head; the unit as the 9 May first writer wrote it (VIER STRIQ DREIZWEIEINS X KLAMMER EIS KLAMMER); ZWEI for 2. The signature has only two X's: FLUGABWEHRKDO SQWARZWALD ROEM EINS CAESAR NR X FRITZ X EINSNEUNNEUN STRIQ VIERNULL GKDOS.

**Method.** This used design S2 (the 14 May design), with a family of four cribs that differ in X layout, run in order and stopping at the first hit.
- The part break was placed by the letter counts of each part before the seal.
- Run 1 gave no candidate (0.99 times the decoy ceiling). Run 2 found the key.

**Prereg.** Hash `65d028eac3ca77e71f7ae4b1efbc24044004424a59aa9a9d22b8db056ee013f7` (13 Nov 2026 05:26:19 UTC). It is timestamped at 05:26:20 UTC by the same three authorities, before the run began at 05:26:57.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 501 against a decoy ceiling of 306 (1.64 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.874, with 9 of 12 words.
- The held-back name decrypts at 0.786 against 0.357 for the best of 200 random key pairs; WARZWALD reads letter for letter.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 192 letters, against 20.

**Reading.** 4 of 283 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked: two letters of one group swapped in writing it down, and two b's read as h.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 05:25 UTC on 13 Nov 2026.

#### The message

*Radio text, with word division*
GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE X VIER STRIQ DREIZWEIEINS X KLAMMER EIS KLAMMER HAT AM X ZWEIFUENF X FUENF X VIERNULL X ST[E/F]LLUNGSWEQSEL VON X KARLSR[U/N]HE X NAQ X OFFENBURG X GEMAQT UND IST SEIT X ZWEISEQS X FUEN[F/E] X VIERNULL X NULLFUENFNULLNULL X UHR FEUER[B/H]EREIT X ‖ FLUGA[B/H]WEHRKDO SQWARZWALD ROEM EINS CAESAR NR X FRITZ X EINSNEUNNEUN STRIQ VIERNULL GKDOS

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. 4./321 (Eis) hat am 25.5.40 Stellungswechsel von Karlsruhe nach Offenburg gemacht und ist seit 26.5.40 05.00 Uhr feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 199/40 g.Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. On 25 May 1940, 4./321 (railway) moved from Karlsruhe to Offenburg, and has been ready to fire since 05.00 on 26 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 199/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 283 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 1 place where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), on the session log (claude-opus-5-5 within two minutes of the run's end).
