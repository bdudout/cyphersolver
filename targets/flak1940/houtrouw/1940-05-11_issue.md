## 11 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1062–1065

**Documents covered.** One message in four parts, one per radio form: headed 196 (frame 1062), 158 (frame 1063), 167 (frame 1064) and 137 (frame 1065). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1061 (SRO 0103, accepted 15.20, passed on 15.45; F 133/40 geh.). Three hand-drawn strokes on it mark the part breaks.

**Part 1 begins with an indicator group; parts 2–4 do not.** Their first group is cipher text. Part 1 is in one hand, parts 2–4 in another.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (25)  11 8 19 25 16 13 4 20 15 1 22 7 14 21 2 9 18 5 23 12 3 24 10 17 6
B (21)  7 16 13 10 20 1 14 8 18 4 9 17 11 2 6 15 3 21 12 5 19
```

**Result.** All four parts read: 653 letters. Two letters on the sheets do not make their words: DREIZWEIEINC (a c-like form where the word wants s) and SIIT for SEIT.

**What it says.**
- 4./321 (Eis) moved on 10 May from Bad Krozingen to Hugstetten station, and has been ready to fire since 03.15 on 11 May.
- The former Wager platoon, in position opposite Rheinhafen-Lauterburg, is disbanded; its guns returned to their units.
- The searchlight sections of 3./861 and 1./861 rejoined the command on 10 May, ready to illuminate since 18.10.

It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day. The same battery is in the 2, 9 and 26 May messages (in this thread).

**The sender's spellings.** LEUQTBEREIT; ROEM X EINS X C for "I c", with NR X F X before the number and GEH at the end; RHEINHAFEN STRIQ LAUTERBURG; ZURUECK with CK.

**Method.** The same search (key lengths 15–30, stage-1 margin 1.10), with parts 1–3 searched and part 4 held out.
- **The first sealed attempt missed.** It dropped the first group of every part, as on every day read before it. With part 1's crib in the 26 May writer's layout it found the key lengths, 25 and 21, twice, but not key A.
- **The second sealed attempt changed one thing**: it read the first group of parts 2–4 as cipher text. Before it ran, the letter counts had pointed there. With those groups included, the cribs of parts 2–4 fit their parts to within one letter, at L1 4, 6 and 3; without them, every crib was 4–5 letters too long. Run 1 found the key.

**Prereg.**
- First attempt: hash `f813ad3e01d08871622b570d5f1793c1eccd269368677ebde44a218e7984ea5c` (13 Nov 2026 06:15:39 UTC), timestamped at 06:15:39–40 UTC by the same three authorities, before its runs began at 06:16:06.
- Second attempt: hash `76c776bf7724fc0cfbbf2af52230c825ab69edb1f2580b39035a2a8707254ba3` (06:31:59 UTC), timestamped at 06:32:00 UTC, before its run began at 06:32:29.
- The blind reading of the sheets is timestamped at 06:03:00, before the teleprinter copy was read.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 970 against a decoy ceiling of 526 (1.84 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.971, with 9 of 12 words.
- Part 4, held out, decrypts at 0.971 against 0.219 for the best of 200 random key pairs, with 11 of 12 words.
- A second decoder agrees. On 30 common German trigrams the keys score 39, against 19 for the best of 1,000 random key pairs.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 510 letters, against 42.

**Reading.** 8 of 653 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 06:14 UTC on 13 Nov 2026; the ledger was unchanged (c8c4e500) when fetched again after 06:42. Dudout's list of first attempts does not include these frames.

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. 4./321 (Eis) hat am 10.5.40 Stellungswechsel von Bad Krozingen nach Bahnhof Hugstetten durchgeführt und ist seit 11.5.40 03.15 Uhr feuerbereit. Scheinwerferstaffel der 3./861 und der 1./861 sind seit 10.5.40 zu Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald zurückgekehrt und sind seit 10.5.40 18.10 Uhr leuchtbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 133/40 geh.

*English*
Secret command matter. On 10 May 1940, 4./321 (railway) moved from Bad Krozingen to Hugstetten station, and has been ready to fire since 03.15 on 11 May 1940. The former Wager platoon, in position opposite Rheinhafen-Lauterburg, is disbanded; its guns have returned to their units. The searchlight sections of 3./861 and 1./861 have been back with Flak Command Black Forest since 10 May 1940 and ready to illuminate since 18.10 that day. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 133/40, secret.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,705 (13 Nov 19:43:41 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 653 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 2 places where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic). The run (06:32:29–06:34:03 UTC) falls in a 37-minute gap in the session log, cut by the tool's compaction; the log shows claude-opus-5-5 on both sides of the gap.
