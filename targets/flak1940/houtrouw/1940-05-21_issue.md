## 21 May 1940: key of the day · frames 0963–0970

**Documents covered.** One message in six parts, headed 193, 192, 211, 194, 202 and 110. Part 3 continues on the back of its sheet (0967). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 0963 (SRO 0202).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (25)  17 21 9 23 14 5 19 25 22 1 18 11 6 15 3 12 8 24 2 16 7 20 4 10 13
B (21)  5 13 19 3 11 8 14 17 2 20 9 4 12 7 21 15 1 6 16 10 18
```

**Result.** All six parts read: 1,070 letters, plus 2 lost at the end of part 5 (headed 202, the sheet holds 200, no back filmed; the words give E and E). Nine letters on the sheets do not make their words: WEUN, VVN, NELN, SEID, DAEI, NUBL, SETVON, DEREIT, SNREIFEN.

**Method.** The same search was run on parts 1–4 alone, with parts 5 and 6 held out. The crib was cut at the hand-drawn part breaks on the teleprinter copy and fitted to the letter counts before the seal.

**Prereg.** Hash `59eb14fd7dcc9620a76b379f9956c24f11202317ebbfb5b07f26abde9e85f515` (12 Nov 2026 21:55:26 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 1,495 against a decoy ceiling of 791.
- Parts 1–4 agreed at 0.968, with 12 of 12 words.
- Held-out parts 5–6 agreed at 0.964; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.209.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 825 letters, against 56.

**Reading.** 8 of 1,070 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 21:52 UTC on 12 Nov 2026.

II/1./942 changed position on 15 May 1940 from Tumringen to target square 256434 a/c (Hagen). Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 183/40. Secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,619 (13 Nov 02:29:54 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 1,070 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 9 places where the sheet's own letter does not make its word; the 2 lost letters are supplied from the words.
