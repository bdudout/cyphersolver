# 15 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 1038, SRO 0139, accepted 15 May 1940 08.55, passed on 10.15)

Secret command matter. 1./35 moved on 14 May 1940 from Kuppenheim to north of Rastatt, and has been ready to fire in its new position since 04.40 on 15 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 151/40, secret command matter.
