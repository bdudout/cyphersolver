# 20 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### Message A (teleprinter copy: frame 0977, SRO 0193, accepted 20 May 1940 16.00, passed on 19.15 and 20.05)

IV/2./861 changed position on 18 May 1940 from east of Scherzheim to target square 655419 d and has been ready to fire since 00.30 on 19 May 1940. 4./351 (F) changed position on 19 May 1940 from Forchheim to map reference R 347530 H 545260, 1 km south of Mingolsheim, and has been ready to fire since 18.50. II/10./351 changed position on 19 May 1940 from Ettlingen to map reference R 347610 H 545285, south-east of Mingolsheim, and has been ready to fire since 17.30. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 181/40. Secret command matter.

#### Message B (teleprinter copy: frame 0996, SRO 0189, accepted 20 May 1940 11.30, passed on 14.25 and 14.35)

Secret command matter. On 18 May 1940 Flak Command Black Forest was given I and III Platoons of 3./932, coming from Flak Group Friedrichshafen; they are placed under Battalion 942. I Platoon target square 256425 b, III Platoon target square 255729 a/b. Both platoons have been ready to fire since 11.00 on 18 May 1940. I Platoon 2./932 changed position from Obertüllingen to target square 256522 d and has been ready to fire since 13.00 on 18 May 1940. 4./321 (Eis) changed position on 19 May 1940 from Hugstetten to Karlsruhe and has been ready to fire since 04.25 on 20 May 1940. II Platoon 10./351 and 4./351 changed position on 19 May 1940 in the Mingolsheim area; details to follow. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 180/40, secret command matter.
