# 14 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 1048, SRO 0132, accepted 14 May 1940 15.35, passed on 16.55 and 17.00)

Secret command matter. 2nd Platoon of 4./491 moved on 13 May 1940 from Titisee to Hausach station, and since 05.00 on 14 May 1940 has been ready to fire, protecting army unit 701. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 141/40, secret command matter.
