# 24 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message

Secret command matter. On 23 May 1940, 3./251 moved from Elgersweier to target square 842865 b (south of Ichenheim); it has been ready to fire since 02.00 on 24 May 1940. III./3./942 and IV./2./942 returned to their old positions on 23 May 1940 and have been ready to fire since 20.30 and 16.00. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 193/40, secret command matter.
