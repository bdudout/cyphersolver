# 9 May 1940: English translation, by Jacob Houtrouw (issue #602), copied from the issue as posted on 14 Nov 2026

#### The message (teleprinter copy: frame 1077, SRO 076, accepted 9 May 1940 09.55, passed on 11.35 and 12.35)

Secret command matter. 4./321 (Eis) has been back with Flak Command Black Forest since 9 May 1940, and has been ready to fire in its new position at Bad Krozingen since 05.10. Four searchlights of 861 and two searchlights of 4./491 have been in position at E.-Hafen Eutingen, ready to fire, since 20.00 on 8 May 1940. 1st Platoon of 2./851 has moved from Leutesheim to the Altenheimer mill, 1,500 metres east of the position of 3rd Platoon of 2./851, and has been ready to fire in its new position since 02.15 on 9 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 125/40, secret command matter.
