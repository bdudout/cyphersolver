All four parts read (683 letters; one letter on the sheet does not make its word). Part 3 has a struck-through first group standing where the indicator stands; the 174 letters after it are text. Found by the same search on parts 1, 2 and 4, with part 3 held out. **The first run, with a crib made by rule, found nothing; the key came out on the one rerun allowed beforehand, after the crib was fitted to the letter counts (month as MAI, heading GEHEIME KOMMANDOSAQE).** Prereg hash `2f41a76af28d591f3729fd30d087799d47b32c8418a745b78da39dbc762fddbe` (12 Nov 2026 00:34:18 UTC). The real search scored 765 against a decoy ceiling of 545; hold-out agreement 0.860 against 0.207 for the best of 200 random key pairs; NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 526 letters against 37. 7 of 683 letters first read differ from what the key wants; 6 changed after a second look, marked. No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images. Prior work: none found at 00:25 UTC on 12 Nov 2026.

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. II. Zug Leichte Res.-Flakbattr. 453 hat Stellungswechsel von Freudenstadt nach Forbach-Gausbach Bahnhof zum Schutze einer Heereseinheit gemacht und ist seit 27. Mai 40 feuerbereit. Der zusammengestellte Zug in Stellung südl. Rheinweiler wurde am 28. Mai 40 aufgelöst, die Geschütze kehrten zu ihren Einheiten zurück. IV. Zug Fla.-MG-Res.-Komp. 305 hat Stellungswechsel von Hechingen nach Planzeiger R 3464760 H 5352850 westl. Hochmössingen gemacht und ist seit 28. Mai 40 18.40 Uhr feuerbereit. Flugabw.Kdo. Schwarzwald I c, Nr. 209/40 g.Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. II Platoon, Light Reserve Flak Battery 453, changed position from Freudenstadt to Forbach-Gausbach station to protect an army unit and has been ready to fire since 27 May 1940. The composite platoon in position south of Rheinweiler was dissolved on 28 May 1940; the guns went back to their units. IV Platoon, Reserve Anti-Aircraft Machine-Gun Company 305, changed position from Hechingen to map reference R 3464760 H 5352850 west of Hochmössingen and has been ready to fire since 18.40 on 28 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, No. 209/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,507 (12 Nov 01:38:18 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.
