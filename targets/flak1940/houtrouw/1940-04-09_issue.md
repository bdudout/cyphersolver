## 9 April 1940: key of the day · frames 1186–1187

**Documents covered.** One message in five parts, typed by teleprinter: headed 180, 142 and 196 (frame 1186), 177 and 204 (frame 1187). It came in to Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald from Luftgaukommando VII ("+ A SMU 359 9/4 1430", SRO 176, received 20.35 on 9 April), as the ledger's 17 May message on frame 0993 did. The plain copy, typed after deciphering, is frame 1185; frames 1188–1189 give the text in longhand. Neither marks the part breaks.

**Every part begins with an indicator group** (JKHWL, BTRPN, MRZAS, RKTOW, QYSZA), each struck through by hand on the sheet.

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

B (27)  22 7 14 26 6 10 18 4 13 17 8 23 15 3 27 9 25 19 11 2 24 5 20 16 1 12 21

**Result.** All five parts read: 874 letters. Seven typed letters do not make their words: RTRIQ, ROEM S SIEBEN (an S for the X), SVEBEN, KLAMMEC, FLUGABWEKR, RQULSQIESSEN and KEAMMER. Two words are a letter short in the sent text itself: GESCHUTZE and BEREIQ DER.

**What it says.** Readiness for action is to be raised.
- The firing at Rust-Oggau is broken off, and its crews and guns return to their units.
- The sound-locator training ordered under Luftgaukommando VII no. 12350/40 of 6 April 1940 will not take place; its locators and crews go back into service.
- Training courses and practice firings in the Flak commands' areas go on, except those of the light Flak groups, which are to man their positions in full.

It agrees with the plain copy; what is new is the key of the day, and the sender's spelling of its office.

**The sender's spellings.**
- GEHEIMEKOMMANDOSAQE at the head, where 17 May has GKDOS; EINS, ZWO and DREI KLAMMER for 1.) to 3.), where 17 May has ERSTENS and ZWOTENS; DOPPELPUNKT, as on 17 May.
- CH in part 1's ordinary words (EINSATZBEREITSCHAFT, SCHIESSEN, ABZUBRECHEN, BEFINDLICHEN, GESCHUTZE), and Q from part 2 on.
- STRIQ for a hyphen (RUST STRIQ OGGAU, HORQER STRIQ AUSBILDUNG), and BRUQSTRIQ for the slash of 12350/40.
- The office as ROEM X EINS X KLEIN ANTON KLEIN OTTO PAULA X ZWO X KLAMMER GROSS ANTON KLAMMER: "Ia op 2 (A)", with the case of each letter.

**Method.** The same search (key lengths 15–30, stage-1 margin 1.10), with parts 1–4 searched and part 5 held out.
- The copy marks no part breaks, so the letter counts placed them before the seal, from each part's rare letters (W, V, Z and H). The cribs then fit parts 1–4 to within 0 to 5 letters each.
- The counts asked for CH in part 1, DOPPELPUNKT and BRUQSTRIQ. After a first fit, the list of variants was widened with the 17 May forms of the same sender, from this ledger's reading of frame 0993; that changed only the held-out part's crib.
- A second run with key lengths 8–35 was sealed in case April's key lay outside 15–30. It was not needed.

**Prereg.** Hash `ec83d5b944cae59bf0d2de3cc89bf8c296ed6ad94985c45fefe0acc5eb0af9ec` (13 Nov 2026 15:18:15 UTC), timestamped at 15:18:15–16 UTC by the same three authorities, before the run began at 15:18:31. The blind reading of the sheets is timestamped at 14:53:11, before the plain copy was read.

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 1,124 against a decoy ceiling of 641 (1.75 times).
- The search text agreed at 0.966, with 11 of 12 words.
- Part 5, held out, decrypts at 0.789 against 0.231 for the best of 200 random key pairs, with 12 of 12 words. Its crib lacked the spelled-out office, which is why the match is lower.
- A second decoder agrees. On 30 common German trigrams the keys score 89, against 26 for the best of 1,000 random key pairs.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 742 letters, against 53.

**Reading.** None of the 874 letters first read is a misreading; the seven letters that break words are typed so on the sheet.

*German*
Geh. Kdos. Zur Erhöhung der Einsatzbereitschaft wird befohlen: 1.) Das Schießen in Rust-Oggau ist abzubrechen. Die dort befindlichen Bedienungen und Geschütze kehren sofort zu ihren Einheiten zurück. Sonderbefehl betr. Inmarschsetzung ist an Schießstab des Luftgaukommando VII in Rust-Oggau erteilt. 2.) Die gem. L.G.Kdo. VII Nr. 12350/40 geh. vom 6.4.1940 befohlene Horcher-Ausbildung findet nicht statt. Die danach herausgezogenen Horchgeräte und Bedienungen sind sofort wieder einzusetzen. 3.) Die bisher durchgeführten Ausbildungs-Lehrgänge und Schulschießen innerhalb der Bereiche der Flugabwehrkommandos werden fortgeführt. Abzubrechen sind nur die Schulschießen der leichten Flugabwehrgruppen. Diese sind sofort voll in ihren Stellungen einzusetzen. Luftgaukommando VII, Ia op 2 (A).

*English*
Secret command matter. To raise readiness for action, it is ordered: 1) The firing at Rust-Oggau is to be broken off. The crews and guns there return at once to their units. A special order on their setting off has been issued to the firing staff of Luftgaukommando VII at Rust-Oggau. 2) The sound-locator training ordered under Luftgaukommando VII no. 12350/40 (secret) of 6 April 1940 will not take place. The sound locators and crews withdrawn for it are to be put back into service at once. 3) The training courses and practice firings held so far within the areas of the Flak commands continue. Only the practice firings of the light Flak groups are to be broken off; these groups are to be put fully into their positions at once. Luftgaukommando VII, Ia op 2 (A).

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.
