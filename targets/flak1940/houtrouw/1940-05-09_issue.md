## 9 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1077–1081

**Documents covered.** One message in four parts, headed 177, 182, 185 and 190, one part per frame; two hands (1078–1079, 1080–1081). The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1077 (SRO 076, accepted 09.55, passed on 11.35 and 12.35; Briefbuch-Nr. F 125/40 g.Kdos).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (17)  4 7 12 5 15 2 10 6 16 3 13 1 8 17 11 14 9
B (20)  13 10 6 18 2 20 15 11 7 16 3 9 14 5 19 1 12 8 4 17
```

**Result.** All four parts read: 714 letters. Eight letters on the sheets do not make their words (one of them unclear, m or n). The sender's own text also has gaps that no reading can explain, because a transposition keeps every letter: in part 4 the "2" of 2./851 is missing, FUENF lacks its N, the year stops at EINSNEUN, and "F R" stands before the number.

**The sender's spellings.** ZWEI for 2 throughout, the year in full (EINSNEUNVIERNULL) in part 1, E X HAFEN, METER, C alone for "I c", G X KDOS.

**What it says.** 4./321 (Eis) is back with the command and ready to fire at Bad Krozingen; four searchlights of 861 and two of 4./491 are in position at E.-Hafen Eutingen; I. Zug 2./851 has moved from Leutesheim to the Altenheimer mill. It agrees with the teleprinter copy, so no unknown text was read; what is new is the key of the day.

**Method.** The same search was run on parts 1–3 alone, with part 4 held out. The crib was cut at the three hand-drawn part breaks on the teleprinter copy and fitted to the letter counts before the seal.

**Prereg.** Hash `86d37c7c8b742a000d5d774885c88a38882f47f265946e79cab20b89ddd60d72` (12 Nov 2026 23:13:55 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 793 against a decoy ceiling of 631 (1.26 times; the bar set beforehand was 1.25).
- Parts 1–3 agreed at 0.948, with 11 of 12 words.
- Held-out part 4 agreed at 0.857, with 9 of 12 words; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.235.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 432 letters, against 37.
- On trigrams the held-out part ties with the best random key pair (10 against 10), so that check adds nothing for part 4.

**Reading.** 6 of 714 letters first read were misreadings in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 22:58 UTC on 12 Nov 2026; Dudout's list of attempted frames does not include 1076–1082.

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. 4./321 (Eis) ist seit 9.5.1940 zu Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald zurückgekehrt und ist seit 05.10 Uhr in neuer Stellung Bad Krozingen feuerbereit. 4 Scheinwerfer von 861 und 2 Scheinwerfer von 4./491 sind seit 8.5.40 20.00 Uhr in Stellung E.-Hafen Eutingen feuerbereit. I. Zug 2./851 hat Stellungswechsel von Leutesheim nach Altenheimer-Mühle 1500 Meter ostwärts der Stellung des III. Zuges [2.]/851 durchgeführt und ist seit 9.5.19[40] 02.15 Uhr in neuer Stellung feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, F 125/40 g.Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. 4./321 (Eis) has been back with Flak Command Black Forest since 9 May 1940, and has been ready to fire in its new position at Bad Krozingen since 05.10. Four searchlights of 861 and two searchlights of 4./491 have been in position at E.-Hafen Eutingen, ready to fire, since 20.00 on 8 May 1940. 1st Platoon of 2./851 has moved from Leutesheim to the Altenheimer mill, 1,500 metres east of the position of 3rd Platoon of 2./851, and has been ready to fire in its new position since 02.15 on 9 May 1940. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, F 125/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,619 (13 Nov 02:29:54 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 714 letters reproduce the plain text above, apart from the 8 places where the sheet's own letter does not make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), by my record: I switched the session to Opus 5.5 after 01:16 UTC on 12 November, before this plan was sealed. No session log survives from these hours.
