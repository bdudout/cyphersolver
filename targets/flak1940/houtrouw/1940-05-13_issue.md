## 13 May 1940: key of the day · frames 1051–1054

**Documents covered.** One message in three parts, headed 183, 169 and 168, one part per frame. The clear teleprinter copy is frame 1051 (SRO 0126, accepted 18.00, passed on 18.40 and 19.50; Briefbuch-Nr. F 138/40 g.Kdos).

**Key** (key numbers per column; the plain text is written under A first, then under B):

```
A (23)  16 20 4 10 14 5 11 22 7 15 1 21 6 23 19 3 17 8 13 2 18 9 12
B (19)  5 13 9 17 3 14 8 19 11 6 16 2 10 15 4 7 1 12 18
```

**Result.** All three parts read: 505 letters. No letter on the sheets fails to make its word.

**What it says.** Two moves of 12 May: I. Zug 9./351 from Thiengen to Rottweil, ready to fire since 21.45; IV. Zug 2./942, ready in its new position at Thiengen since 14.05. It agrees with the teleprinter copy; what is new is the key of the day.

**Method.** The same search was run on parts 1–2 alone, with part 3 held out. The settings were as for 14 May: key lengths 15–30 and a stage-1 margin of 1.10. The crib was cut at the two hand-drawn part breaks and fitted to the letter counts before the seal (L1 4, 2 and 0).

**Prereg.** Hash `0499f7644b281a20d3bf4b9900405e79abe32929f76610d3f73c321fa11f825b` (13 Nov 2026 00:58:50 UTC).

**Checks.**
- Stage 1 scored 828 against a decoy ceiling of 406 (2.04 times).
- Parts 1–2 agreed at 0.985, with 12 of 12 words.
- Held-out part 3 agreed at 0.963, with 12 of 12 words; the best of 200 random key pairs reached 0.215.
- NARA's page text fits the predicted groups at 397 letters, against 33.

**Reading.** 1 of 505 letters first read was a misreading in the first transcription, changed after a second look and marked.

**Not done.** No human has compared the transcription or the reading with the images.

**Prior work.** None found at 00:54 UTC on 13 Nov 2026. Dudout's list of first attempts does not include these frames.

*German*
Geheime Kommandosache. I. Zug 9./351 hat am 12.5.40 Stellungswechsel durchgeführt von Thiengen nach Rottweil und ist seit 12.5.40 21.45 Uhr feuerbereit. IV. Zug 2./942 hat am 12.5.40 Stellungswechsel durchgeführt und ist seit 12.5.40 14.05 Uhr in neuer Stellung (Thiengen) feuerbereit. Flugabwehrkommando Schwarzwald I c, Nr. F 138/40 g.Kdos.

*English*
Secret command matter. 1st Platoon of 9./351 moved on 12 May 1940 from Thiengen to Rottweil, and has been ready to fire since 21.45 on 12 May. 4th Platoon of 2./942 moved on 12 May, and has been ready to fire in its new position (Thiengen) since 14.05 on 12 May. Flak Command Black Forest, I c, no. F 138/40, secret command matter.

**How the key was obtained:** from plaintext beside the ciphertext (the clear copy of the same message on the reel), as described above.

**Hash list in Bitcoin.** The day's sealed plan is in a hash list whose OpenTimestamps proof is anchored in Bitcoin block 975,631 (13 Nov 04:20:34 UTC, the block's own time). The anchor proves the plan existed by then; that it was sealed before the run rests on the RFC 3161 timestamps where they are given, and otherwise on the run's output files and file times.

**Checked again before filing.** On 14 Nov 2026 a decoder written separately, in another session, deciphered this day again from the transcription and the key: all 505 letters reproduce the plain text above, with no place where the sheet's own letter fails to make its word.

**Model.** Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), by my record: I switched the session to Opus 5.5 after 01:16 UTC on 12 November, before this plan was sealed. No session log survives from these hours.
