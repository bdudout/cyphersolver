# AGS, Patronato Real, leg. 52, doc. 129 (PARES description 2207852; viewer show/2207852, 20 images).
# Spanish clear text, Ferdinand and Isabella to Dr De Puebla, Tortosa, 28 Dec. [1495]. Foliation 692-699.
# Transcribed by Brice Dudout (with Claude), 14 Nov 2026, from the PARES images (about 970 x 1350 px), enlarged.
#
# Method: two independent passes. Pass 1 was read first. Pass 2 was blind: it did not see pass 1, the folder
# notes or any edition. Differences were settled on the image. Both raw passes are in this folder
# (doc129_pass1.txt, doc129_pass2_blind.txt).
# THIS FILE = the arbitrated text of pp. 1-2 (images 1-2), the closing lines, date and subscription (image 15),
# the old marks and the endorsements (image 20). Pages 3-14 (images 3-14) are not arbitrated yet: use the two
# raw passes, which agree on the outline but have many doubtful words.
#
# Semi-diplomatic. Spelling as written (u/v, i/y, ç). No punctuation added. [ ] = expanded abbreviation.
# {x} = doubtful; {x|y} = the two passes differ and the image does not settle it; ‹…› = unread.
# <del>…</del> struck through; <ins>…</ins> interlinear addition. The scribe writes "en" as a looped,
# circled sign; it is printed here as "en" (it gives sense everywhere: "en salvamento", "en ningund lugar").
# No decryption of doc. 124 was attempted and doc. 124 was not compared with this text, except for the clear
# lines already printed in sample_transcription.txt (see README.md).

## Pages 3-14: not arbitrated (see doc129_pass1.txt and doc129_pass2_blind.txt). Outline, read in both passes:
# p. 3 "aquel que se llama hijo del Rey Duarte", the duchess Margaret in Flanders; p. 4-5 the King of Scots,
# "fiadores de los mercaderes", marriage of one of the Infantas with the Prince of Wales; p. 6 "procurar que se
# faga la amistad del Rey de Romanos e del Rey e la Reyna de España e del Rey de Ynglaterra e del Archeduque";
# p. 7-8 the Archduke, the county of Flanders, breaking war with France, the marriages of the Prince of Spain and
# of the daughter of the King of the Romans; p. 9 the duchess Margaret; p. 10-11 the treaty said to be made by
# France with Venice and Milan, the League, the King of Naples; p. 12 papal bulls sent with Salvador Duarte,
# couriers drowned at sea; p. 13-14 the King of France's dealings, the ambassadors in Scotland.
