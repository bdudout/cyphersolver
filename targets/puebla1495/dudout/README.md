# The Spanish clear text AGS PTR leg. 52 doc. 129 (issue #609)

Contributed by Brice Dudout (with Claude), 14 Nov 2026. Record: AGS, Patronato Real, leg. 52, doc. 129, PARES
description [2207852](https://pares.cultura.gob.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/description/2207852), viewer
show/2207852, 20 images. The images were read on PARES and are not in this folder.

## The record

- 2 bifolia, foliated 692-699: text on images 1-15, blank versos 16-19, endorsements on image 20.
- Old mark: printed **"T .c. Y. 2"** at the head of every recto, the same mark as on doc. 124. On the dorso an
  archival hand writes it out as **"T. con Ynga. / Leg. 2"**, which is Bergenroth's "T. c. I. L. 2" (Tratados con
  Inglaterra, legajo 2). Also on the dorso: "T .c. Y. 2 / 26" and "& 609".
- Date line (image 15): **"… de Tortosa a xxviij de dizienbre de noventa e {seys}"**. The year word is doubtful.
  Pass 1 reads "seys", the blind pass reads "çinco". A later note in the margin and the endorsement both write
  "1496" and give it as 1495 ("28 de dic.e 1496 segun hoy 1495"), which fits "seys" in Nativity style.
- Subscription (image 15), in the hand of the text: **"yo el Rey / yo la Reyna / por mandado del Rey e de la
  Reyna / Ferrand Alvarez"**. These are not autograph signatures: the text is a copy or a minute.
- Endorsements (image 20). A later archival hand writes: "Copia de carta de los Reyes catholicos a su Embaxador en
  Ynglaterra / faltale el principio / Descifrada / negocios de Ingalaterra, Escocia, Flandes, Françia y Rey de
  Romanos", signed {Pablo} Tamayo. A faded 19th-century note reads "Copiose … Ariz y Silva en 16 Julio de 1859".
  In a contemporary hand, written the other way up: "Copia de carta de sus altezas que truxo ‹…› .ix. de {março}
  de .96. y {rescibio} ‹…› a su Embaxador".

## Is it the clear of doc. 124? (external features only)

We did not decrypt doc. 124, and we did not compare it with this text. The only comparison is with the clear
lines already printed in `../sample_transcription.txt`.

- **Same text where doc. 124 is in clear.** Doc. 129 image 1 l. 16 to image 2 l. 10 ("partio de aca se le
  escrivio a catorze de mayo … con Salvador Duarte … la conpania no vos ha podido escrevir … los fatores de la
  conpania … con la corte aqui a Tortosa … ternan cargo dello") is word for word the clear passage of doc. 124
  p. 2 ll. 2-6. The only differences are spelling and the dates, which doc. 129 writes as numerals. One verb is doubtful in
  doc. 129: "escrivio", or possibly "respondio".
- **Same place, date, addressee and cover.** The letter is from Tortosa, dated 28 December. It is addressed to
  "el dotor de Puebla" (endorsement: "a su Embaxador en Ynglaterra"). It uses the same merchant cover as doc. 124:
  "la conpania", "los fatores", "mercaderes", "mercaderias".
- **Incipit.** Doc. 129 starts in mid-sentence: "{para} que fagamos hazer {o fazer} guerra al Rey de Françia".
  The endorsement says "faltale el principio". The lost opening is the part of doc. 124 that has no clear text
  here. Our report in #609 put the join at doc. 124 image 1 l. 22; that was not re-checked today.
- **What kind of copy.** The external features point both ways:
  - Like a chancery draft: lines are struck out and rewritten. On image 2, "que se llama hijo del Rey Duarte" is
    struck and replaced by "aquel que esta en Escoçia".
  - Like a decipherment at the other end: the margin of image 2 has "{esto esta} {errado|çifrado} por las {letras}
    y no se entiende"; the endorsement has "Descifrada"; the contemporary endorsement reads "copia … que truxo …
    .ix. de {março} de .96.".
  - Not settled either way: whether it was made from doc. 124 itself or from another copy (no. 113 mentions three).
- Doc. 130 (PARES 2207853, same date and PARES title) is a second clear text of the same despatch. We have not
  transcribed it. One of the two is probably Bergenroth's "clear draft"; the 1859 copy noted on doc. 129 fits
  his years at Simancas.

## Files

- `doc129_transcription.txt`: the **arbitrated** text of pp. 1-2 (images 1-2) and p. 15 (end, date,
  subscription), with the old marks and the endorsements.
- `doc129_pass1.txt` and `doc129_pass2_blind.txt`: the two raw passes over all 15 pages. Pass 2 was made without
  seeing pass 1, our notes or any edition. Pages 3-14 are not yet arbitrated. The two passes agree on the outline
  of each paragraph, but individual words are often doubtful: use them as a guide, not as a reading.

Conventions: semi-diplomatic; abbreviations expanded in [ ]; {doubtful}, {pass 1|pass 2} when the two passes
differ and the image does not settle it; ‹…› unread; `<del>`/`<ins>`. The scribe writes "en" as a looped,
circled sign; we print it as "en".
