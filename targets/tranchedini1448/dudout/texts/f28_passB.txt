Notes on readings
- R1 "t": a back-slash with dots (Lasry t). "ri": h-shaped sign, stem with bowl and tail (Lasry ri); same shape in L6, L7, L10, L12, L14.
- rb? (L5 3rd sign, L9 before ss): a 5/ʒ-like sign whose top curls to the right and whose bowl ends in a tail to the lower left; distinct from the
  straight-stemmed h-shaped "ri" but could be the same letter. Could also be "re" (ς) — I kept "re" for the ς-with-stem signs of L7.
- pi (L5, L13): a V whose left arm sweeps up in a curve and whose right arm ends in a hook, with an inner stroke (∀-like); read as Domnina pi, could be "to".
- tt: in every case the cross has its two dots on the LEFT side, one above and one below the left arm (÷ joined to +). If the table distinguishes dd, check.
- Ap? (L5): light cursive A with a long left tail, no dot; could be Abold.
- L6 "s?": a plain stem with a bar to the right at mid-height (⊢), no flag at the top.
- L7 "n" after li: loop on stem, TWO dots to the right (one above, one below the bar); I used n (dots right only), not ne.
- L7 "pa?": a cluster of four small loops, no stem (compare @B03, which is three loops on a stem).
- L8 "oo+?": two loops (first one curly, like a cursive a), then a cross whose bar joins the loops; could be o2 + e or ce + e. Same shape in L12 ("ꝏ+").
- L9 "b6d?": a 6 with a long top bar and a dot inside the upper left; "rho?": a long bar ending in a ρ-loop whose tail runs down-left (a stroke from another line may cross it).
- L10 first sign "e?": a cross with a small curl at the top of the stem.
- L10 "t2?": small × (back-slash crossed by a short stroke) with a dot at the upper right; L14's two "t" are a back-slash with a dot and a short dash, closer to Lasry t. They may be the same sign.
- L11: after "pregham" an "o" joined by a hairline to the COSIMO frame; I took it as the clear ending "preghamo" (if cipher, it would be "asm" before COSIMO).
- L11 "ño": looks like ordinary script (n or h with a stroke above + o) between "a" and the Λ-with-dot; I split R7/R8 there. If "ño" is cipher, R7+R8 is one run.
- L11 "tu Abold": a Λ under a long top bar followed by an A; could be a single ligature.
- L12 first "asm?": small "o" after "mayo", separated by a space; possibly the end of the clear word.
- L12 "o3 o3?": first is a small ɣ with a tick at top left; second is a larger ɣ whose arms cross at the top (8 open on top) — could be i2.
- L13 "o2?": a dot followed by the curly ɔc. "fa?": a bar followed by a compressed double loop.
- L14 "nu?": an undotted circle, a bar crossed by a short vertical tick, ending in a slashed loop, with a dot under the bar; could be oc + rho.
- After the last "to" (L14) a cursive flourish (not a clear "=" double hairline) ends the cipher; not counted.

New shapes
@B01 — capital H (two stems joined by a mid bar), = Lasry 1452 "si" shape; not in the legend. L5 (after ro), L12 (after oo+).
@B02 — a long horizontal stroke from the left crossed at its right end by three vertical stems (a comb, Ш-like, whose stems pass through
       the bar); resembles IN (Ш on a bar) or a turned X3. L6, first sign after "nouelle p(er)".
@B03 — three small loops in a clover (two above, one below) on a short stem going down; = Lasry 1452 "pe" shape. L10, before MIL.
@B04 — b with a long top bar (as ss) followed by dots above and below a bar to the right (÷), i.e. "ƀ÷"; = Domnina "na" shape. L12, after @B01.
@B05 — Z-like: a top bar running left from the head of a stem, and a foot bar running right from its base (⌐ over ⊥); compare Lasry r third form. L13, between n and mi.
