#   texts/sign_legend.md. Value hints in the legend and the key pictures were not used; the labels below are names for shapes only.

#         show-through from the recto.
# Method: text-line centres from a horizontal ink-projection profile. Each line was read in 650-px windows at 2.4x, and doubtful
#   signs were checked at 3.2-4x (crops in /Volumes/SPOF_2/Archives-Veille-cyber/tranchedini1448/crops_f30/).
#   L<n> = text line n counted from the first written line of the page. y = approximate pixel centre in the full image.
#   A trailing "?" marks a doubtful reading. [tick] = a small check-like stroke after the last sign of a run (punctuation?), not counted.
#   The clear words are copied as written, with abbreviations left unexpanded or only roughly expanded.

## Notes on labels as applied (shape decisions)
- dd = dotted cross with the dots on BOTH sides of the stem (upper-right and lower-left). tt = dotted cross with both dots on
  the LEFT arm, one above and one below it (÷ on the left arm).
- ss = b under a long top bar that crosses the stem (T over b). "ss?" = the same, but the bowl ends in a long horizontal tail to
  the right. co = the same family with a dot to the left of the stem. b6d = the top bar starts at the stem and runs right only,
  with a dot beside the stem (L10, once).
- ri = b-shape with a hook or descender at the foot. rb = 5-like b with the top bent or hooked to the left. Several rb/ri calls are
  marked "?".
- ci = small m with an upright stem rising from it. A plain small m with no stem is @N15.
- n = loop on top of a stem, with a bar to the right and a dot under the bar. ne = loop to the RIGHT of the stem top (p-like), with
  a bar to the right and a dot above and a dot below the bar.
- nu = circle, then a bar ending in a small loop, with a long oblique stroke descending to the lower left. The circle has a dot
  only at L14; elsewhere no dot is visible ("nu?").
- COSIMO is used for the frame with an I inside: open-bottom ΓIΓ on the verso, closed rectangle with an inner I on the recto
  (L8, L14, L15, L44).
- CON = Ч / 4-shape with a stem (L29, verso L15).
- o2? = a plain double loop ∞ with no curl (L15, L16, L17, v.L16), as distinct from the curly ɔo.
- Recurring sequence: trefoil @N03 immediately followed by a cross e (L10, L21, L26). I read these as two signs.
  The cross does not cut through the loops, so I did not use oo+.

## New shapes (labels invented here; N06, N09, N31 and N32 were retired during the work and are unused)
@N01  Top bar with a stem whose bowl hangs on the LEFT of the stem (ɖ under a bar; mirror of the T-over-b family). Once, L5.
@N02  Ribbon: a loop on top with two crossing legs, the right leg often extended (like li but with NO dot under it). L5, L7, L13, L16, L17, v.L15.
@N03  Trefoil: three loops (8 with a third loop at the right). Frequent.
@N04  Capital R, written exactly like the R of the clear script (cf. "R. poy" on L5). Inside R1 at L6; it may be a clear "R(e)" left in the cipher.
@N05  r joined by its bar to a double stem crossed by the same bar (r#). Once, L6.
@N07  Closed triangle with a bar running out to the right (∆—, a 4-like triangle). L7, L12, L15 ×2, L17, L18, L21, v.L16. Not the legend's mi (-▷).
@N08  Plain diamond ◇ pierced by a vertical stem above and below; no inner loops. Once, L7. Possibly a variant of ma.
@N10  V with an inner stroke or small inner loop (∀-like but with a stroke inside the V). L7, L8, L9, v.L15.
@N11  Two short stems standing on one long bar (⊥⊥, a 2-prong comb). L10, L23?, L26, L27.
@N12  Π with a stem rising from the middle of its top bar. Once, L10.
@N13  N-shaped sign, heavy strokes. L10, L17, L18.
@N14  H (two uprights joined by a bar). L11, L13, L14, L29, L44.
@N15  Plain small m with no stem. L6 ×2, L10, L11, L14, L15, L44, v.L17.
@N16  7-shape: top bar with the stem dropping from its right end, the stem crossed by a short bar (⁊+). L11, L17.
@N17  Loop on top of a plain stem (♀ / ϙ), with no bar and no dots. L12, L18 ×2, L43, v.L15, v.L17.
@N18  Three loops stacked vertically (a tall 8 with a third loop). L12, L15, L22, L26, L44, L45, v.L15, v.L16 ×2.
@N19  Horizontal bar whose left end turns down (a foot), crossed by an upright stem (⅃+). L12, L16, L25, L26?, L45.
@N20  < (left-pointing angle) with a small circle at each open end and a dot between them. L12, L13, L14, L22, L29, v.L15.
@N21  Heart: two loops above a downward point (ma without the top spike). L16, L17 ×2, L21.
@N22  Small h/n with a tall raised left stroke (ɦ-like, no bar). L18, L44.
@N23  Bar, then a pointed diamond, then a bar, with a spike rising from the diamond top (g whose loop is a diamond; possibly g2). Once, L21.
@N24  Two looped stems joined by a horizontal bar (H whose uprights each carry a loop). Once, L21.
@N25  ɔ: an open reversed c, sometimes with a short bar to the right. L21, L22, L45.
@N26  d-shape: a bowl on the left of a tall stem that also descends. In the damaged spot of L23 (doubtful).
@N27  nu-shape (circle, bar, small end loop, long descending stroke) crossed by an upright stroke with a dot below the bar. Once, L24.
@N28  b with a long horizontal tail to the right and no top bar (b—). L21, L25.
@N29  Double loop (ꝺo-like) struck through by three oblique strokes; possibly a cancelled sign. Once, L29.
@N30  U with a dot inside, its left arm hooking over to the left and its right arm running on as a long top bar over the next sign. Once, L29.
@N33  π: a bar over two legs. Once, v.L16.
@N34  Plain < (mirror of q), heavy. Once, v.L17.
(Uses of "blot" (v.L17): a heavy cross overlaid by two oblique strokes, with a filled centre; doubtful.)

## The two shapes asked about
(a) Bent F with a foot: NOT found as such. The only F-like sign is uu, at L25 (a straight stem with two bars to the right and
    no foot). The nearest "bent with a foot" shape is @N19, a bar whose left end turns down, crossed by an upright stem. It
    occurs at L12 (x≈2900), L16 (x≈680), L25 (x≈1410), L26 (x≈2050, doubtful) and L45. If (a) means a cross-like sign with a
    bent foot, @N19 is the candidate.
(b) Inverted triangle / V with a small circle on each top corner (FIOR): YES, exactly once, at recto L14 (y≈1365, x≈1565),
    between de and te, in R4. Related but rotated: @N20, a "<" with a small circle at each open end plus a dot. It occurs 6
    times (L12, L13, L14, L22, L29, verso L15).
