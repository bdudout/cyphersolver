# Labels are those of ../../vestigia/sign_inventory.md (T = inverted T, UPT = upright T, n/m = square brackets,
# C = reversed c, o = circle with tail, P = cup, R = barred sign, F = stem with bar and loop, X = H shape,
# TT = two strokes between dots, G = barred G/q sign, W = cross with small circles, d = letter d, 3E = reversed E,
# PI, LAMBDA, E = the 4-like sign with a long tail), plus shapes this letter has and the queen's letters do not:
#   5    = figure 5
#   DH   = dotted hook: a descending curve with a blob at the foot and a detached dot at the upper left (9-like)
#   9    = the 9-form with a curled or looped head (in this hand the loop is open, so the head looks like a 2)
#   9u   = 9-form whose head (curl or dot) cannot be told on the web image
#   O0   = small o crossed by a long slanting stroke
#   B    = square bracket [ (serifs top and bottom); L = plain capital L. The two are not always separable,
#          and neither is the L with a hooked top that the earlier key of this letter gave to g. All L-forms
#          are written L or B here by their serifs only.
#   Zs   = small z-like sign (once)
#   NK NX = two large signs after "regina" (K-like, X-like with a dot); NTRI = triangle between dots;
#   NFERD = crossed square; NSTAR = six-armed star; NBAR, NH = an upright crossed stroke and an H-like double
#          stroke that stand inside two words with no letter of the copy to match

# Notes on single places.
# - line 8 mittere, sign 2: a small z-like sign where the copy has i; elsewhere i is 3 or O0. Perhaps a hasty 3.
# - line 13 modum, sign 2: a circle with a tail (the l sign) where the copy has o; line 16 writes the word with PI.
# - line 14 terras, signs 3-4: two shapes where the copy has rr, neither the usual R; not read.
# - line 15 scripsimus, sign 5: PI-like where the copy has p (line 21 scripsit has the cup P).
# - line 18 sentibus: the copy of the time reads the first sign as s; the sense wants gentibus. Same L-form in
#   congregabit (twice) and excogitare where the copy has g.
# - line 18 plures, sign 3: blotted.
# - lines 19 and 21: NH and NBAR stand before the second half of congregabit and of disposituros.
