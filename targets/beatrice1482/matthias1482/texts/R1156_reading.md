# R1156, Matthias Corvinus to Ercole I d'Este, Pozsony, 1 June 1482: reading

The whole letter, written out 23 Oct 2026 (translation wave v7) from the two decryption files of the entry: the clear
text as printed by Fraknói 1895 no. 131 (`texts/R1156_fraknoi1895.txt`, from the Modena original; the Hungarian OCR's
stray accents on Latin vowels are dropped, its broken words joined and its letter misreadings set right:
apparaturii apparatum, ludicio Iudicio, nuUo nullo, vei vel, ei'ga erga, jíro pro; its notes and editorial "(így)" left out), and
the cipher runs as read here from the glyphs (`texts/R1156_decryption.md`), set in ⟨ ⟩, which correct the print in
four places (valent, pluribus gentibus, venient, the closing sentence). Code signs as there: `[⅋ = Venetorum]`,
`[ꝗ = Rex Hungarie]`, `[⊠ = regi Ferdinando]`; `[ꝗ]` where its value is uncertain (*quod* or *con-*); `[2 signs]`
unread. The last sentence (the regest) is in cipher on the letter; Fraknói prints it as a contemporary note at its
foot. Latin.

Revised 14 Nov 2026 (round 15) on Vestigia's images of the cipher original (document 2626) and of the clear copy made
at the time (2627): *electos* added after *quingentos* (in the cipher and in the copy, absent from the 1895 print and
from the first reading here); `[ꝗ]` after *significet* is *Regi Hungarie* as the copy has it, and the sign after
*faciet* is the ordinary clear abbreviation of *quod*, not a code sign; more runs are marked as cipher (the first
reading gave them as clear text); *gentibus* is written with the s-sign and the copy reads *sentibus*; the name signs
for the Emperor, King Ferdinand and the Duke of Ferrara are given as `[sign = ...]` with the copy's words. Sign
transcription: `vestigia/R1156_V2626_transcription.txt`.

## Reading

Mathias Dei gratia Hungarie, Bohemie etc. rex, illustrissimo principi, domino Herculi Ferrarie ac Mutine duci etc.
salutem et prosperos ad vota successus. Ex litteris illustrissime domine ducisse, consortis vestre, sororis et cognate
nostre carissime, intelleximus statum rerum vestrarum et apparatum Venetorum contra vos, quod libenter non audivimus;
quod serenissimus dominus rex noster et pater noster carissimus et alii domini et confederati vestri sint vobis
auxiliaturi, et quod iam de facto ceperint; nos quoque pro singulari benivolentia erga vos non deerimus ea facere que
vobis profutura cognoscemus. Nam lectis litteris statim cum domina regina consorte nostra statuimus ⟨mittere⟩ in
⟨subsidium vestrum quingentos electos equites⟩, inter ⟨quos⟩ essent ⟨centum usarones⟩, idest ⟨homines⟩ levis
⟨armature⟩, qui plus ⟨ceteris valent⟩. Sed cum examinaremus cum domina regina [2 signs] et ⟨aliis⟩ nostris per ⟨quam
viam⟩ possemus ⟨eos mittere⟩, non invenimus aliquam ⟨tutam⟩. Iudicio nostro essent enim ⟨transituri⟩ per ⟨loca⟩
[sign = imperatoris], cum quo in presentia ⟨conflictamus⟩; sed de hoc non multum curaremus, quia bene inveniremus
⟨modum transeundi tutum⟩; sed cum haberent ⟨transire⟩ per ⟨terras⟩ [sign = Venetorum], veremur quod nullo modo
⟨transire⟩ possent. ⟨Scripsimus⟩ [sign = regi Ferdinando], ut [sign = rex ipse] vel [sign = dux Ferrarie] possent
aliquem ⟨excogitare modum⟩ quo ⟨tuto mitti⟩ possent; libenter ⟨mitteremus⟩. Non dubitamus quod fideliter et bene
servirent. In crastinum hinc recedet ⟨Mager Balas capitaneus noster⟩ cum ⟨pluribus gentibus⟩, iturus in ⟨Croatiam⟩
versus ⟨confinia⟩ [sign = Venetorum], ubi ⟨plures⟩ etiam ⟨congregabit⟩; et quotienscunque inventa erit ⟨tuta
transeundi via⟩, statim significet [sign = Regi Hungarie] et illic faciet quod ⟨venient⟩. Speramus cito nos ⟨res
nostras⟩ ita ⟨disposituros⟩, ut meliori postea modo ⟨vos iuvare⟩ possemus.

[sign = Rex Hungarie] ⟨scripsit⟩ [sign = regi Ferdinando] quomodo possent ⟨diverti⟩ [sign = Veneti] et ⟨confundere
eos⟩ et cum paucis ⟨pecuniis⟩. Datum Posonii, primo Junii 1482.
