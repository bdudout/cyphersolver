“Matthias Corvinus to Ercole I d’Este, 1 June 1482” (DECODE R1156) is in the same cipher key as the two letters Queen Beatrice wrote to Ferrara in the same ten days. On 14 November 2026 the two values on which the keys seemed to differ were checked on the image of the king’s letter and agree. One key now has one page:

  • Beatrice d’Aragona to Eleonora and Matthias Corvinus to Ercole I d’Este, Pozsony, May–June 1482 (the king’s letter is section 04; the check of the key is in section 05)
